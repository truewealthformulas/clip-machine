# The rulebook (Agent 1 writes by it, Agent 2 enforces it)

The Success Club posts Kevin Trudeau clips, with his permission, to Instagram,
Facebook, YouTube Shorts and TikTok. The goal: give real value in every clip,
so viewers follow and go to the free training link (Kevin's official
"Your Wish Is Your Command" page, with our affiliate code). We are an affiliate,
so **we are legally responsible for every claim in our own posts.**

## A. What makes a good clip
1. **Stands alone.** Opens on the setup or the want; ends on the payoff line. A
   stranger with no context must understand it. The last sentence is the punchline.
2. **Methods and stories beat warnings.** "How rich people buy houses" earned 30-40x
   the shares and saves of "A mortgage is a scam". Pick teaching moments, stories,
   step-by-step methods, memorable phrases.
3. **Length 30-58 seconds.** Over 60s, YouTube's Content ID blocks the Short and an
   Instagram Story is rejected. Never cut mid-sentence; never end on a setup
   ("...and that's why...").
4. **No ads, sponsor reads, phone numbers, "call now", product pitches or
   "go to my website" segments inside the clip.** Pure teaching only.
5. **Distinct.** No two clips from the same episode about the same point.

## B. Hooks (the on-screen headline)
- 6 words or fewer, at most 2 lines, Title Case.
- Starts with a promise word: How / Why / What / When / Where / Which / The / A /
  An / a number word, or contains a number.
- Never a riddle: never starts with Some / There is / Nobody / Everyone / He / She /
  They / I / It is / This is what.
- No full stop at the end. No question mark riddles. No clickbait that the clip
  doesn't deliver.

## C. Captions
- **The first line is the hook** - it is all most people read before "more", and on
  YouTube it becomes the title. 40-90 characters, a promise or a tension the clip pays
  off ("Most people never ask this one money question." / "Why rich people say how,
  not can't."). Never start with "Ad", "Kevin Trudeau on", a hashtag or an emoji.
- Then 2-4 short paragraphs telling the idea (credit him naturally: "Kevin explains ..."),
  ending on ONE question to the viewer ("Which one do you say more often?").
- Last line: hashtags, and it MUST start with **#ad** (affiliate disclosure - the post
  promotes his offer, so the disclosure stays, at the end instead of the start).
  Example: `#ad #mindset #success #kevintrudeau`. Max 5 hashtags including #ad.
- 300-700 characters total.
- **No links, no URLs, no @handles, no "link in bio", no "comment X"**. The machine
  adds the right call to action per platform automatically. Adding one by hand
  creates duplicates and gets accounts flagged.
- Must not copy the on-screen hook word for word.

## D. Claims we never make (platform policy + FTC + Kevin's history)
Kevin was found in contempt and imprisoned over misleading marketing claims. Every
caption and hook must avoid:
- **Income or results promises:** "make $10K a month", "become a millionaire",
  "guaranteed", "get rich fast", "double your income", "financial freedom in 30 days",
  "passive income", specific money amounts promised to the viewer.
  (What Kevin says about his own life inside the video is fine; promising
  the viewer an outcome in OUR text is not.) **No money amounts at all in hooks
  or captions** ($, "10K", "a million dollars") - the checker rejects them.
- **Health or medical claims:** cure, heal, disease, cancer, diabetes, depression,
  anxiety treatment, weight loss, lose weight, detox, "never get sick", doctors lie,
  drugs/pharma conspiracy. Any clip whose main point is a health/medical claim is
  DROPPED, whatever the caption says.
- **Conspiracy / fear bait as the hook or caption focus:** "they don't want you to
  know", "secret society", "the elite are hiding", "banned", "the government".
- **Fake urgency / scarcity:** "only today", "last chance", "before it's deleted".
- **Personal attributes targeting** (Meta policy): "Are you broke?", "Are you
  depressed?", "You're overweight". Speak about ideas, not the viewer's
  finances/health/body.
- **Engagement bait** (Meta/TikTok demote it): "like if you agree", "share to win",
  "tag 3 friends", "follow for part 2".
- Profanity, hate, harassment, politics, religion attacks, sexual content.
- Impersonation: never write as if we ARE Kevin ("I", "my book"). We are a club
  sharing his teaching.

## E. Platform limits the machine relies on
| Platform | Limit we keep |
|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | caption <= 2,200 chars, <= 5 hashtags, video <= 90s (stories <= 60s) |
| Facebook Reels | caption <= 2,200, video 3-90s |
| YouTube Shorts | title <= 95 chars (first caption line becomes title: make the first line 20-90 chars and meaningful), video <= 60s for licensed material, <= 15 hashtags or YouTube ignores all |
| TikTok | caption <= 2,200, no links (under 1K followers), video >= 3s |

## F. Posting rhythm (Agent 3 watches)
- 5 posts a day on the main account (slots 08:00, 11:30, 14:30, 17:30, 20:30 by default).
- Max 2 clips from one episode folder per day, 45 minutes minimum between posts.
- Every episode gets its own folder (brand), so the day mixes episodes.
- Keep at least 7 days (35 clips) queued.
