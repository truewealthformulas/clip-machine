Clip Machine

The Success Club's private publishing tool: short clips with success principles, published to The Success Club's own social accounts.

How it works

1. Prepare. Short vertical clips are cut, captioned and given a cover image.
2. Connect. The account owner signs in with each platform (Log in with TikTok, Google, Meta) and grants permission to post to their own account.
3. Review. Before a clip goes to TikTok, the owner sees the video, the caption, the connected account, and chooses who can view it and whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed. Promotional posts are labelled.
4. Publish. The clip is sent to the owner's own account at the scheduled time. Nothing is posted to anyone else's account.

Clip Machine has one user, The Success Club. It is not a public service and does not collect data about other people.
