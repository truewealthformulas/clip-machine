Privacy Policy – The Success Club (Clip Machine)

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Clip Machine is a private tool used only by The Success Club (truewealthformulas@gmail.com) to publish short video clips to its own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts.

What data the app uses

The app signs in only to The Success Club's own accounts. It uploads videos and reads basic statistics (views, likes, shares) for those videos. It does not collect, store or share personal data about any other person.

How data is stored

Access tokens are kept as encrypted secrets in a private settings store and are used only to publish and measure The Success Club's own posts. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is used only to upload and measure videos on The Success Club's own channel.
